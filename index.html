I am a PhD candidate in Philosophy at UC Berkeley, advised by John MacFarlane and Seth Yalcin. I work in philosophy of language, epistemology and philosophical logic. My research focuses on what conditionals sentences mean, what it is for something to matter, when evidence supports a conclusion, and what you can and cannot do with probabilities.

Before my PhD, I was a Fulbright scholar at Berkeley and MIT. I did my MA at the Munich Center for Mathematical Philosophy (LMU), where I studied with Ivano Ciardelli, and I am now an external member of his ERC project on inquisitive modal logic at the University of Padova. I did my undergraduate degrees in mathematics and philosophy at the University of Oslo.
